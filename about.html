Safe AI Bremen.

We’re a local community for people in Bremen who want to understand the risks of advanced AI and what we can do about them.

The questions we ask

Can we tell when a system will fail?

An AI model can pass a test and still make mistakes in everyday use. How can researchers design tests that reveal those gaps before deployment?

Who gets to decide what is safe enough?

Releasing a system affects people beyond the team that built it. What evidence should developers share, and who should review it?

Who belongs here?

You might come to these questions through computer science, public policy, philosophy or your own experience using AI. You’re welcome to start with a question you haven’t found an answer to.
