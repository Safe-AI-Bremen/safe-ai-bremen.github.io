When you visit the site

When you visit the site on GitHub Pages, your browser sends GitHub your IP address and the file it is requesting. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. Our legal basis for delivering the site is our legitimate interest in making it available securely and reliably (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub bases retention on the purpose of the processing and applicable legal obligations. It does not specify a fixed retention period for Pages IP logs in its documentation. Opening a local copy of this site requires no connection to GitHub.

The site serves its own fonts, images and scripts. We use no analytics, tracking cookies or third-party embeds. Following an external event or resource link takes you to the linked provider, whose privacy policy applies there.

  • How GitHub Pages handles visitor data

When you contact us

You can write to either founder. When you send an email, the founder you address receives your email address, any name you provide, your message and any attachments. Google provides our Gmail mailboxes and processes the message for delivery and storage. Contact is voluntary. We need a return address if you would like a reply.

We use correspondence to answer your enquiry and coordinate any follow-up you request. That is our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We keep correspondence for as long as needed for that purpose, or longer if a legal obligation or a legal claim requires it. If you write by post, AI Safety Aachen e. V. may receive the envelope at the address above for forwarding to Valeriya.

  • Google’s privacy policy

International transfers

GitHub and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. For transfers to participating US organisations, the providers describe relying on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. For other transfers they may use the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. The providers explain which safeguards apply and where to find them.

  • GitHub’s privacy statement
  • Google’s data transfer safeguards

Your rights

You can ask Valeriya for access to your personal data, correction, erasure or a restriction on processing. Where the legal conditions apply, you also have a right to data portability. Use the contact details above to exercise your rights. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, including the one in Bremen.

  • Bremen data protection authority’s complaint form

Your right to object

You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interests (Article 21 GDPR). We will stop that processing unless we can demonstrate compelling overriding grounds or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
